Features

Sab kuch jo ek student ko chahiye

Har feature specifically Pakistani CS students ke liye design kiya gaya hai — language se le kar learning style tak.

💬

Roman Urdu Explanations

AI explains bugs the way you actually talk — Roman Urdu mixed with English terms. Not cold error messages, but a dost-style explanation.

🫖

Desi Analogies

Every error comes with a Pakistani everyday analogy — chai, rickshaw, biryani, darwaza — so the concept actually sticks in memory.

🔧

Auto-Fixed Code

Complete corrected code with line-by-line annotation. Not just what changed — but WHY every change was made.

📊

Mistake Pattern Tracker

Tracks your most common error types across sessions — turns a debugger into a personalised learning coach.

🧒

Bachon Wala Mode

New! Extra simple explanations for absolute beginners. Short sentences, zero jargon, maximum encouragement. "Ghhabrao mat!"

📚

Concept Roadmap

After every fix, recommends the exact concept to study with a direct YouTube search link. Fixes bugs AND gaps in knowledge.

⚡

Zero Setup

Single HTML file. Open in Chrome. Works. No npm, no pip, no accounts, no subscription. Just open and learn.

📤

Shareable Result Cards

New! Generate a WhatsApp-ready image of your explanation. Share with friends so they learn from your bug too.
